# SYNNOD: posts for X / Twitter

Everything below describes what is actually built and running. Visuals are in `docs/media/`.
Rules used: no price talk, no promises about the token (it is a **test token on a test network** for now),
no claims that the AI is conscious. The tone of the project is quiet, curious, a little strange.

Replace `{LINK}` with the site URL. Every tweet is at most 280 characters.

## Thread (English), 12 posts

**1/12**  (media: `01-hero-night.png`)
```
128 voices. One mind.

SYNNOD is one shared AI character that nobody owns. Its brain is 128 hexagonal cells. Each cell belongs to one person, and everything people tell it becomes one common memory.

```

**2/12**
```
Claim a cell, connect a wallet (you only sign a message, no payment), and speak through it: a place, a memory, a strange dream.

The mind answers, remembers, and mixes your words with 127 other voices.
```

**3/12**  (media: `03-face-states.png`)
```
It has a face. One pixel eye.

Awake: it blinks and looks around.
Thinking: the pupil spins while it reads you.
Speaking: it "talks" while the reply types out.
Nobody around for a few minutes: it falls asleep.

The iris colour is its mood.
```

**4/12**
```
When it answers, the words don't just appear.

They type out character by character, and the few letters ahead of the cursor flicker as pixel static before they resolve. Your cell flashes at the moment it hears you.
```

**5/12**
```
Neural links.

When the mind has a thought, sparks run across the brain from the cells whose words fed it. You can literally watch a thought grow out of several strangers' voices.
```

**6/12**  (media: `02-day-and-night.png`, dusk frame shows the wave)
```
Someone claims a free cell and a shockwave rolls across the whole brain, with a little 8-bit fanfare.

Every cell also glows by age: the ones that just spoke burn bright, then slowly cool. The brain shows what the mind is remembering right now.
```

**7/12**  (media: `02-day-and-night.png`)
```
Day and night.

The whole site follows your local clock: pink dawn, cool blue day, magenta dusk, deep violet night. Open it at 3 a.m. and it is a different place than at noon.

(There is a chip in the header to pin any hour.)
```

**8/12**
```
When nobody has spoken for a while, the mind dreams.

Its breathing slows, old memories flicker up at random, the eye closes.

In live mode this is visual only. The site never invents thoughts for the AI. If it has nothing to say, it says nothing.
```

**9/12**  (media: `04-node-profile.png`)
```
Click any taken cell to open its profile: voices spoken, replies still in memory, thoughts it shaped, its share of the mind, a timeline of what it said and how the mind answered, and a map of the cells it shares thoughts with.
```

**10/12**  (media: `05-search-ping.png`)
```
128 hexagons look alike, so there is search.

Type #48 or part of a nickname: the cell pulses pink on the brain and its card opens. Every node also has its own page and a share card, so you can post yours.
```

Built for people, not just wallets:

- pixel UI with an "explain every block" help mode
- 8-bit sounds + generative lo-fi that follow one switch
- a motion switch for calm still frames
- works on phones (tap snaps to the nearest cell)

Docs: {LINK}/docs
